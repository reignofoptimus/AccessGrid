# AccessGrid surfaces
Mode: Operate. Scope: login, administrator, security and student workspaces equally.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A campus access desk: one shared frame, three distinct working surfaces. Replace the pale generic cards with a focused dark workspace whose hierarchy follows the user's task.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal surfaces, warm white text, a muted yellow-green action color, self-hosted Manrope and tabular plate identifiers. Thin rules, compact square controls and generous page-level space. Dark chosen for extended gate-monitoring use, including evening shifts; that use scene is a design assumption, not a deployment claim.

STORY: Sign in, recognize the role, inspect the relevant vehicle or campus state, and act without leaving the working context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 224px rail, compact utility bar, large contextual title and a four-column data band. Admin sees a wide parking schematic and activity ledger; security sees plate lookup and its decision panel; student sees a personal vehicle pass and parking availability. Login uses an oversized typographic introduction, a geometric gate-access demonstration, and a quiet dedicated form.

FORM: Code-first by user choice. Systemic/data direction selected from campus operations; no seed key was produced because the Impeccable engine is unavailable. The launcher and seed command were attempted. No claim of an assigned or approved generated comp.

SIGNATURE: A plate reveal when lookup succeeds, matched by a short state transition on navigation. All motion has a reduced-motion fallback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
